---
title: Open data
summary: Where FROST's datasets will be shared, so that others can build on them.
date: 2026-10-01
---

FROST is committed to open science. All data and results will be made openly accessible, so that other researchers can test, reuse and build on them during and after the project. Datasets will be deposited in trusted, open-access repositories:

| Data | Where it will be shared |
|---|---|
| Speleothem isotope and trace-element records | [SISAL](https://pastglobalchanges.org/science/wg/sisal/intro), [NOAA Paleoclimatology](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology) and [PANGAEA](https://www.pangaea.de/) |
| Pollen records | [European Pollen Database](https://www.europeanpollendatabase.net/), [Neotoma](https://www.neotomadb.org/) and [PANGAEA](https://www.pangaea.de/) |
| Radiocarbon dates | Radiocarbon database of the Royal Institute for Cultural Heritage (KIK-IRPA), Brussels |
| (Crypto)tephra finds | Published tephra databases |
| Other palaeoenvironmental data | Continental and global databases |

All FROST journal articles will be published **open access**. See [Publications](../publications/).
